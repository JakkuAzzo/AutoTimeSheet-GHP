Pump Repairs in London

GMT Electrical Services receives pump repair, supply and installation enquiries at its Croydon workshop, including for sump, pool and multi-stage equipment. Provide the pump details, application and fault symptoms; the workshop will assess the request and advise on the appropriate next step.

Pump equipment and faults

When contacting GMT, describe the pump type and what it is used for. Let the team know what has changed and whether the pump is at the workshop or at a site.

  • Pump make and model, if known
  • The system or equipment it serves
  • Visible damage, leaks or changes in operation

Repair, supply and installation

The suitable route depends on the pump type and application. GMT will review the equipment details and advise whether a repair, replacement supply or installation enquiry is appropriate. Scope and availability are confirmed for each request.

  • Sump pumps
  • Pool pumps
  • Multi-stage pumps and other commercial equipment
