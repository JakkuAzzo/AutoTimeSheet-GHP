Motor faults to describe

A clear description helps GMT understand the repair enquiry. Include what the motor is connected to and the symptoms noticed before it stopped or changed performance.

  • Overheating, unusual noise or vibration
  • A motor that will not start or run as expected
  • Recent changes in use or operating conditions

Repair, rewind or replacement

The appropriate route depends on the motor and its condition. Provide the make, model, nameplate details and fault symptoms. GMT will assess the information and advise whether repair, rewind or replacement should be considered; scope, availability and any quotation are confirmed before work proceeds.

  • Provide make, model and nameplate details
  • Include photos if they help identify the equipment
  • Confirm availability and the next step with the workshop
