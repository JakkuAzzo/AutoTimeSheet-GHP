Our story

A Croydon engineering company since 1985

GMT Electrical Services was started in 1985 by three partners who had worked for an experienced company in the electrical engineering industry. From its Croydon base, the company has continued to adapt while building experience across motor rewinds, motor repairs, pumps, fans, kitchen extractors and gearboxes.

Today, GMT supports businesses with workshop and site enquiries, and with repair, supply and installation options. The team’s approach is practical: understand the equipment and the fault, then discuss a suitable way forward.
