Microsoft Lists is the authoritative task record. Staff can submit requests and see tasks they requested or have been assigned. Accounts and Operations can approve, reject, assign, schedule, or cancel work. Assigned staff move approved tasks through To-Do, In-Progress, and Completed.

Every change is sent through the protected Microsoft 365 workflow and appears in the task history after confirmation.
