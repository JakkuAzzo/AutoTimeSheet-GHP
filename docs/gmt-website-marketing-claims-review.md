# GMT Website Marketing Claims Review

This is an internal pre-publication checklist for claims that need GMT confirmation. New or unconfirmed areas are currently phrased as enquiries, with scope and availability to be confirmed directly with GMT. Do not change those to promises of capability or performance until the supporting facts are confirmed.

## Confirm before adding to public copy

- Emergency and 24-hour response availability, same-day support, and any promised turnaround time.
- Motor scope by AC/DC type, voltage, phase, power range, ATEX or flameproof capability, and any compliance or OEM-standard wording. The AC/DC pages currently invite enquiries and say GMT should confirm the options for each motor.
- Specific testing, load testing, core-loss testing, balancing, cooling-system, component manufacture and machining capability. The balancing and component pages currently ask customers to contact GMT and confirm scope.
- Collection, free local delivery, courier, geographic coverage beyond the existing London and South East description, and availability of on-site work.
- Cost comparisons, savings, energy-efficiency percentages and any claim comparing repair with replacement.
- Named industries and customer sectors GMT wants to claim as served.

## Evidence needed for customer proof

- Case studies: confirm the job record, equipment, outcome, dates and permission to publish each customer story. The draft examples in the supplied brief are not published.
- Testimonials and logos: obtain the exact approved quotation or logo and permission to use the customer’s name or mark.
- Before-and-after gallery: provide paired photographs of the same equipment/job, verify the repair record and approve publication. Current public photography is presented only as workshop imagery.
- History: confirm founder names, workshop milestones and any additional company history before expanding beyond the existing account of GMT’s 1985 founding by three partners.
